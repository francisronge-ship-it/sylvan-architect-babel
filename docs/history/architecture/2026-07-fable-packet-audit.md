## Decisions Retained

- Product/workbench, benchmark, and derivational database are separate systems.
- No confirmed-worse derivational output may be accepted for cost, latency,
  formatting, convenience, or implementation simplicity.
- Babel never invents linguistic content. Recovery is inadmissible by default
  and must be bounded, evidence-backed, and content-preserving.
- Full derivations are required; output allowance is not derived from sentence
  length.
- Provider settings are recorded in their native terms. Babel does not
  manufacture temperature or equate providers' effort labels.
- Francis selects models and conditions per benchmark release. Cost is measured
  and reported; it does not silently narrow coverage or review quality.
- Four-field `derivationStages` is the sole authored structural source.
- One immutable record represents one analysis.
- Save creates per-analysis records plus one thin saved-work wrapper atomically.
- Wrappers, notebooks, and collections reference records instead of copying
  derivation bytes.
- Local/private persistence, explicit export/import, integrity checks, and
  quarantine precede sync or publication.
- Benchmark claims advance from development evidence to expert/method-reviewed
  releases; provider tests never become default product verification.
